# M2 Paper Feel Handwritten Notes

Output language: Use English for all subtitles, narration, titles, labels, signs, and other readable text unless the user explicitly requests another language.

- Category: M Dynamic Style · Explanation
- What it looks like: Off-white paper, markers drawn one by one, handwritten words, large bold title words, occasionally the whole picture "pops" and cuts to dark colors.
- Suitable for: listing key points, explaining methods, and "you can do this" paragraphs.

## Style prompt words

Paper-feel handwritten note style. The off-white paper background uses only paper color, ink black and two accent colors for color matching, and charcoal gray is used for dark mode. Marker annotations are drawn one by one, handwritten words are written with stroke-dashoffset, and titles are written in large bold fonts. Come up with a new idea every 1.5 to 2 seconds, and only do one main action per transition. The movements should have realistic easing, squeezing, stretching and following. Do not allow interface elements such as playback bars and timecodes to appear.
