# M10 Turn an abstract concept or emotion into a picture

Output language: Use English for all subtitles, narration, titles, labels, signs, and other readable text unless the user explicitly requests another language.

- Category: M Dynamic Style · Emotion and atmosphere (feeling lines are most commonly used)
- What it looks like: There are no specific people or objects, but changes in shapes, words, and colors are used to express a feeling.
- Suitable for: passages about emotions such as fear, anxiety, uneasiness, and "thinking too much".

## Style prompt words

Make a [15] second motion graphic to express the feeling of "[emotion/concept]", such as "anxiety" and "fear of the unknown". Don’t use specific people and objects, but use changes in shapes, words and colors to express them. Slowly change from [one state] to [another state].
