# M8 spoken + verbatim subtitles

Output language: Use English for all subtitles, narration, titles, labels, signs, and other readable text unless the user explicitly requests another language.

- Category: M Dynamic Style · Emotion and atmosphere (feeling lines are most commonly used)
- What it looks like: White background, one font, subtitles typed word by word following the conversation, paired with real pictures, and the camera slowly drifts.
- Suitable for: the main line of the entire film. For films that are narrated or broadcast verbally, the subtitles must match.

## Style prompt words

The main line is oral recording. The subtitles appear word by word following the speech, and the time is corrected from the recording using Whisper, not word by word according to the text. White background, only one font, black. The lens only drifts slowly once per segment. The picture changes on the 16th note of the music. There should be no periods in the words on the screen. Don’t give the text the same background color as the check box. The music rises at the key sentence, and automatically lowers when the vocals come out. Loudness is unified to -14 LUFS.
