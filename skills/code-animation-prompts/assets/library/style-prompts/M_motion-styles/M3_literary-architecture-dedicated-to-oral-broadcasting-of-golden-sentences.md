# M3 text building (dedicated to oral broadcasting of golden sentences)

Output language: Use English for all subtitles, narration, titles, labels, signs, and other readable text unless the user explicitly requests another language.

- Category: M Dynamic style · Text and golden sentences
- What it looks like: Just words. Each word has a "structural role": some are beams, some are columns, some are floors. The previous words are built up, and the last sentence "stands" on the things built up before.
- Suitable for: the closing sentence "Learn and believe in yourself". The most worthwhile part of the whole film.

## Style prompt words

Make a [20] second, text-only spoken animation. The lines in order are: [Sentence 1], [Sentence 2]... Each keyword has a structural role, such as "learning" is the foundation, "belief" is the pillar, and "self" is the person standing on it. The text is built with real glyph outlines, and each sentence changes the structure of the picture. The last sentence must "stand" on what the previous words have built. Color palette: Dark background, paper-colored text, and an accent color. No cliche images such as microphones, crowds, and flags, no stock videos. After selecting 8 frames for inspection, the order of reading is correct, and the final platform is indeed built up by the previous elements.
