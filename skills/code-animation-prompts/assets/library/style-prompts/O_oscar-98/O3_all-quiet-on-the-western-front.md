## Style prompt words

Expressionist woodcut after Kasei Kollwitz's War series (1921–22). Sixty to seventy percent of the picture is solid black; the shapes are separated with a white outline knife, the width of the knife is 1/300-1/150 of the width of the painting; the human face is carved into white shapes, leaving only a few strokes of black facial features; the hands should be large. The characters fill the screen and are cropped by the frame. Each knife has a sharp entrance and closing edge and a chipped wood edge. The closer the knife is to the light, the wider it is. The knife marks are arranged in groups and the rhythm is slightly uneven. Ink color #17130F is printed on #E8DFCA paper; texture is only wood grain bars and missing ink spots (±5%). Only the protagonist around whom the story revolves wears a color plate (such as #D97757) and lays it flat on the body or clothes. The knife marks are still pressed on it, and black still accounts for more than 60%. Light only comes in through one opening (window, gap in clouds); the lamp only carves a small circle of short knife marks. The ground beneath the characters' feet remains permanently black. Movements are quiet (breathing, a small gesture); rock slowly, push slowly. Avoid: foggy gray tones, rows of knives arranged in stripes, and blood.

English:

Expressionist woodcut after Käthe Kollwitz, *Krieg* (1921-22). 60-70% solid black; white contour cuts 1/300-1/150 of frame width separate forms; faces are white shapes with a few black features; hands large. Figures fill the frame, cropped by its edges. Each cut tapers in and out with chipped edges, is wider the nearer it sits to the light, in slightly uneven bundles. Ink #17130F on paper #E8DFCA; texture is only wood-grain streaks and under-inked speckle (±5%). One colour block (e.g. #D97757) only on the hero the story centres on, flat on its body or clothes, cuts over it; black stays 60%+. Light enters through one opening (window, cloud gap); lamps get a short ring of cuts. The ground under figures stays uncut black. Quiet motion (breathing, a small gesture); slow pans and push-ins. Avoid: grey fog or midtones, even striped hatching, gore.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays Paul and wears a German helmet. The whole section is slowed down (1.2 times) and very quiet: the hand starts to reach out before and after the third beat, and it takes three beats to slowly reach to the end; the phantom soldier only appears in the last shot. Four hard cuts: Face in the trench → Paul’s arm → Butterfly → Cemetery of Crosses.
