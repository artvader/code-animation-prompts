Simplified geometry, dense detail on every built surface (openings with sills, ledges, railings, ladders, strung lines); textured brick, plaster or rock, never plain. Theatrical light: a low warm sun rakes surfaces with long shadows; cool fill a quarter of the key. The one hero subject in saturated orange #D97757, at least 1/6 of frame height, set against dark brick or given a black rim so it never melts into the sky. Puppet-like mannequin figures. Warm, saturated grade, fine grain (±3%). Signature shot: a low angle against the orange sky; then one continuous descent into the set and a ground-level push. Avoid: photoreal grime, violet-pink skies, plain walls, poses that pop.

### Director’s Note

Clawd infiltrates the Jets gang, has no costume, and relies on the scene to identify the film. The finger snaps are stuck on the drum beat; the leap is a clean parabola, from squatting to landing in one smooth motion. The overhead shot at the opening echoes the table array grid in the previous film; the bright match at the end is in the center of the screen, exactly where the sunrise is in the next film, and is edited to match.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Take an overhead shot of the West District neighborhood grid (rooftops, water towers, playgrounds and courts), then lower and lower into the street. (1.3s)
2. From a low camera position on the side of the court, the Jets gang formed a wedge shape in front of the graffiti wall and snapped their fingers, with Clawd in front; a continuous camera zoomed around to show a close-up of Clawd, whose snapping fingers were stuck on the drum beat. (1.7s)
3. An overhead shot from the fire escape: Clawd squats and takes off, a parabola presses on the red sky, and next to it is the dancer's big jump; the camera rises along the arc. (1.2s)
4. Close shot of the fire escape platform: Clawd lights the match, and the camera zooms in until the flame stops in the center of the frame, and is then blown out. (0.8s)
