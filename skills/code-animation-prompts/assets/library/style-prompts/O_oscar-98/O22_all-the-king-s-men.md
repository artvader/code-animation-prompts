## Style prompt words

Each frame is a letterpress poster printed with wood type and block printing. Three colors are printed on the beige paper #EEE3C7: blue #20488C for the sky, water and large dark areas, red #C82C28 for the warm side and stripes, and black #1E1B1E for the outline and text; only add an extra spot color for the protagonist the story revolves around (such as #D97757). One version of each color, offset by 2–4 pixels; the lower layer is hollowed out, and only the dots are overprinted. There are wood grains on the ground, pinholes missing ink, and ink squeezing around the edges. The coarse dots are removed in the middle (about 1/200 of the frame width), and the screen angle is different in each version, no more than one-third of the frame. Hemp glue prints of figures: thick black outlines, white lines of the graver, and shadows of the crescent moon. About 70% of the screen is pictures; one or two lines of short sentences written in rough wooden movable type on the banners and signboards in the pictures, with offset projection, the height of the characters is about one-tenth of the screen, in English unless another language is explicitly requested. Camera: small push, pan, close-up on the retake (no music every 2 seconds). Benchmark Hatch Show Print. Avoid: crushed gray tones instead of large solids, and color registration as accurate as a computer.

English:

A letterpress poster from wood type and cuts. Three inks on cream stock #EEE3C7: blue #20488C (sky, water, shadow masses), red #C82C28 (warm surfaces, stripes), black #1E1B1E (outlines, type); only the hero the story centres on gets a spot ink (e.g. #D97757). Each ink 2-4 px off register; solids knock out the inks beneath, halftones overprint. Wood grain, pinholed starved patches, squeezed edges. Mid tones: coarse dots (about 1/200 of frame width), varied angles, on at most a third of the frame. Figures as linocuts: heavy outlines, gouged white lines, crescent shadows. Picture fills about 70%; short wood-type lines (about 1/10 of frame height, user's language) with offset shadows sit on banners and signs. Camera: small pushes, a lateral track, a bump on each strong beat (or every 2 s). Reference: Hatch Show Print. Avoid: busy grey mid-tones, perfect registration.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays Willie Stark: fedora hat pushed back, red and blue tie loosened, speech notes rolled up in one hand. In the panoramic view, the fist is raised halfway, and the middle shot shows the crowd's response. In the close-up, it steps on the beat and raises the fist to the top, then swings it again. The camera shakes slightly when the punch is punched. All hard cut.
