## Style prompt words

19th century sailors' needle engravings on polished whale teeth. Signature shot: a wide, flat whale's tooth lying across an oak table, occupying about 85% of the width and 70% of the height of the frame, with an inkwell and a pocket knife beside it. Engraved with near black ink #17110c: fine needle line arrangement (line width is about 1/800 of the picture width, line spacing is about 1/500), undulating outlines and stipples. The carved lines cover about 70% of the tooth surface: the sea, the main body and the land are dense, and the sky is lightly lined; the sea is a row of stylized waves. The main character should be large (approximately one-third of the height of the tooth surface) and colored; in addition, red #9a2a22, blue #2b4a78, and yellow #e9b526 are only for embellishment. Warm ivory base, light growth lines, polished scratches, honey yellow tooth tips. Folk simple composition: corded border, streamer inscription, radiating sun, and patterns of the main world. The curvature relies on edge shading and a window reflection to keep the picture from deforming. First, pat the whole tooth, push forward and then slowly move laterally, and the waves will move accordingly. Avoid: Thick lines, solid color fills, and elongated horns.

English:

Scrimshaw: 19th-century sailor's engraving on a whale tooth. Signature shot: a broad, flat-sided tooth lies horizontal on an oak table, about 85% of frame width and 70% of its height; ink bottle, jackknife beside. Engrave in near-black #17110c: needle hatching (lines 1/800 of frame width, spaced about 1/500), swelling outlines, stipple. Lines cover ~70% of the face, dense in sea, subject and land, sky lightly hatched; seas as rows of stylised waves. The hero is large (~1/3 of the tooth's height), coloured; otherwise red #9a2a22, blue #2b4a78, yellow #e9b526 only as small touches. Warm ivory, growth bands, scratches, honey tip. Folk layout: rope border, ribbon motto, rayed sun, motifs from the subject's world. Shade the edges, one gloss streak; don't warp the picture. Push in, then pan slowly as waves animate. Avoid: thick lines, solid fills, a slim horn.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays Ruby, wearing a yellow waterproof bucket hat and standing on a fish box on the bow of the boat. There is only one cut: cutting from the object to the depiction, and then moving sideways slightly, bringing the line of sight from the father's sign language to the singing, and the staff unfolds along with the singing.
