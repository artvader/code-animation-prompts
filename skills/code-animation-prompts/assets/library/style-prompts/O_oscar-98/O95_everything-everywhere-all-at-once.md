## Style prompt words

Real-time light stepping 3D multiverse montage: the subject jumps between three or four universes, each with its own painting method, all transformed from the subject's own world, for example: a realistic workplace under cold green fluorescent lights, full of furnishings; a surreal universe with a gravitational lens halo; a quiet wilderness vista with white subtitles; an advertising product photo with a candy pink background and pink and blue outline light. Pause for 2–4 seconds each and adjust colors individually. The connecting object throughout: a movable eye sticker on the subject, which accounts for about one-tenth of the height of the painting. Jump 0.5–0.8 seconds: Advance this eye, smear, RGB separation, and explode a ring of rainbow color blocks (#e5322d, #f7c928, #1fa39a, #2a6fc0, #e0478f). The ring is filled with movable eyes and pink beads. It expands to about 80% of the height of the painting and then flashes white. Signature image: a rainbow ring blooms around the subject; the first jump takes about 1 second. Refer to Daniels Brothers' "The Instant Universe" (2022). Avoid: The universe is all the same and the rings are too small and plain.

English:

Ray-marched real-time 3D multiverse montage: the subject jumps through 3–4 universes, each its own look, invented from its world, e.g. a realistic workplace under cold green fluorescents, densely dressed; a surreal cosmic version with a gravitational-lens halo; a silent wide landscape with white subtitles; a candy-pink product shot with pink-and-cyan rims. Each holds 2–4 s with its own grade. One link through all: a googly-eye sticker on the subject, about 1/10 of frame height. Transition, 0.5–0.8 s: push into the eye, smear, split RGB, then a rainbow ring of colour segments (#e5322d, #f7c928, #1fa39a, #2a6fc0, #e0478f) studded with googly eyes and pink beads blooms to ~80% of frame height and bursts to white. Signature frame: the ring around the subject; first jump at ~1 s. Reference: Daniels, *Everything Everywhere All at Once* (2022). Avoid: look-alike universes, a small plain ring.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays Xiulian, with big eyes on her forehead and a fanny pack on her waist. Each time the universe jumps, the eye is pushed forward first, and then smear, RGB separation and white flash are performed, stuck at the cutting point; each universe has a different action: standing, stepping on the piano keys with feet, sitting with a stone, and being sucked into a bagel. Finally return to the starting point and open your arms in the mandala.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Panoramic view of the IRS office, with receipts flying everywhere, and the camera pans down the aisle to Clawd; he has a big eye on his forehead and a fanny pack hanging from his waist. (0.8s)
2. Cut to the auditor and her steel desk filled with receipts. She holds up a receipt; camera slows down. (0.6s)
3. Cut to Clawd with his arms raised, the camera zooms into the big eyes on his forehead, and a universe-hopping flash of light explodes. (0.4s)
4. Hot Dog Fingers Universe: A panoramic view of a glossy piano under candy pink light. The hot dog fingers of one hand lie limply on the keys, with Clawd standing on the keys. (0.5s)
5. Cut close to Clawd’s feet, stepping on the keys, and small hot dogs sprouted from the ends of his arms. (0.5s)
6. Push the big eyes again and jump to the next universe. (0.4s)
7. Panoramic view of the silent canyon: two rocks with big eyes, Clawd sitting quietly next to them, subtitle "We're all small and stupid."; the camera slowly drifts along the cliff. (1s)
8. Bagels of all things: Clawd looks up at the huge black bagel. The camera pushes all the way into the black hole in the middle, passing through the horizon. Clawd is sucked forward. (1.7s)
9. Back in the office: Clawd opens his arms, and a rainbow mandala ring with big eyes grows from behind; the camera gently pulls away. (0.6s)
