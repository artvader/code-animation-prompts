# O79 The Departed

The last shot of "The Departed" is of a rat crawling over the railings under the golden dome of the State House. Street stencil graffiti has always loved drawing rats.
- Style: Boston Red Brick Wall Stencil Graffiti
- Keywords: 2D

## Style prompt words

Street stencil graffiti on old red brick wall. Each work is sprayed into flat color blocks using 3-5 hollow templates, giving it a photo-like feel (the minimum hollow is about 1/200 of the width of the painting): hard cuts, soft spray on the tilted parts of the template, scattered spray spots, sagging, and the connecting ribs that must be left on the template. Colorways: Black #151312, Medium Gray #8C8A85, Gold #C99A2E, Red #C3261F; Red Brick #7A3224, main work sprayed on brushed-on cream patches. The unevenness of the brick surface, the shadows of the gray joints and the rain marks are shown through the paint, allowing the paint to eat into the brick joints. Several works are arranged side by side on one wall, with short hand-sprayed slogans about the subject matter (in the language of the audience), old tags and a torn corner of a semi-paste poster. Light: Soft daylight sweeps across the wall diagonally, with moderate contrast, and the shadows of wires sway gently; no vignetting is accentuated. Lens: First let the work spray out layer by layer, then cut to a close-up and move laterally. Compare Banksy. Avoid: Clean edges like vectors; paint floating on bricks; too many colors; dark walls.

---

English:

Street stencil graffiti on an old red-brick wall. Each piece is 3–5 stencils sprayed as flat colour, photo-derived in look (smallest cut detail about 1/200 of frame width): hard cut edges, soft overspray where a stencil lifted, spray dots, drips, stencil bridges. Palette: black #151312, mid grey #8C8A85, gold #C99A2E, red #C3261F; brick #7A3224, cream painted patches under the main pieces. Brick relief, mortar and rain streaks show through, so paint sinks into the joints. A busy wall: several pieces side by side, short hand-sprayed slogans about the subject (user's language), old tags, a torn halftone paste-up. Light: soft raking daylight, mild contrast, a faint swaying wire shadow; no heavy vignette. Camera: pieces spray on layer by layer, then close-ups and lateral tracks. Reference: Banksy. Avoid: clean vector edges; paint floating on the brick; too many colours; a dark wall.

## Replenish

### Technique keywords

Multi-layer spray paint template, Banksy's mouse, red brick wall texture, halftone paste poster

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays an undercover cop with a flip phone pressed to his ear, himself a layer of airbrushed out of the wall. The first two shots were sprayed out layer by layer, and then mobile phones, mice, Clawd hung up the phone, and quick cuts appeared one after another. No one spoke, the bell and the mouse finished the story.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Panoramic view: the entire wall, template paintings are sprayed out layer by layer: city, golden roof, railings, mice, Clawd holding a mobile phone, red slogan; the camera is slightly zoomed out. (0.8s)
2. Close shot: Two spray-painted flip phones are ringing in the air, and Clawd has the phone close to his ear; he listens and glances to the left; the camera slowly drifts to the right. (0.6s)
3. Medium shot: The rat runs along the railing under the golden roof. "WHO'S THE RAT?" is spray-painted in the upper left corner. The red and blue light of the police lights sweeps across the brick wall; the camera then pans to the right. (0.6s)
4. Close shot: Clawd puts the phone down and looks to the right; the camera zooms out slightly. (0.4s)
