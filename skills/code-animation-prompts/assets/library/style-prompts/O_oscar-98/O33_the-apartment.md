### Director’s Note

Clawd as C.C. Baxter: bowler hat, apartment key with orange and red sign (51 W 67), sitting on the edge of the front table. The office lobby is drawn line by line. In the second shot, the key is raised and the camera zooms in. Finally, it cuts to the overhead table array grid, which is graphically matched with the overhead shot of the Manhattan neighborhood grid in the opening of the next film.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. One shot to the end: white lines create an endless office lobby from complete darkness, with gray tones and ceiling lights lit up in rows; Clawd (Baxter) sitting at the edge of the front table holds up the apartment key in the second shot, and the camera zooms in. (1.9s)
2. Cut to the top and look down: The tables are arranged in a grid, Clawd is lying on his back on his table, with the keys and sign in his hand; slowly push closer. (0.6s)
