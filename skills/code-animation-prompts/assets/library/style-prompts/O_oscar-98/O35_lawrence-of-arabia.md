## Style prompt words

Hand-painted epic movie poster from the 1960s, with gouache as base and oil paint as highlight. First, draw the body solidly and then add color: lay down an opaque mid-tone base color, and then use strokes that follow the body (the width of the large surface is about 1/80 to 1/50 of the width of the picture, and use a small brush for the face and hands), with soft edges and smooth edges; each body is divided into three levels: light, medium, and dark; the dry brush is only used on dust, cloth, and distant figures; finally, apply sharp warm cream highlights on the light-receiving edges. Warm and cold contrast: cobalt blue sky #1F4D97 transitioning to light blue #A9C3D6, warming around the low sun; ground #EEC27A (any terrain), red ocher #B56A33 for skin, dirt and clothing, purple #6C6394 for distant shadows. The main character is the largest in the center, and with a diagonal movement, the fabric floats up; there can be smaller and looser figures behind him who retreat far away. Light canvas grain, warm vignetting, fog. Movement: ups and downs, slowly pushing to a close-up angle, shaking the lens to create the picture. Avoid: broken and even scratch lines, flat digital colors, and only cool colors.

English:

1960s hand-painted epic film poster, gouache with oil highlights. Draw forms solidly, then paint: an opaque mid-tone base, directional strokes following each form (1/80–1/50 of frame width on large areas, smaller on faces and hands), soft-blended edges; model every form in three values; dry-brush only on dust, cloth and distant figures; sharp warm-cream highlights last on lit edges. Warm–cool contrast: cobalt sky #1F4D97 to pale blue #A9C3D6, warming to cream near a low sun; ground #EEC27A (any terrain); ochre-red #B56A33 for skin, earth and costume; violet #6C6394 for distant shadows. The hero centred and largest, a diagonal gesture, cloth streaming; smaller, looser figures may recede behind. Light board grain (±3%), warm vignette, haze. Motion: bounce, slow push to a low close-up, a whip pan out. Avoid: scratchy uniform hatching, flat digital fills, cold-only colour.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays Lawrence: white robe, white turban, golden headband, riding a camel and holding a scimitar. It opens with one of the most famous match cuts in film history: the match flames from the previous film turn into a desert sunrise. The entire charge is carried out at a constant speed of 1.5 times. The scimitar is raised and the tip of the knife flashes. Finally, the camera is flicked out of the picture to pick up "Tom Jones".
