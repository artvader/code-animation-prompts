## Style prompt words

Victorian toy paper theater (Pollock's style). Everything is printed cardboard: the black lithographic outline is about 1/400 of the width of the picture, and there are sparse crayon lines on one side of each cutout; the hand-painted colors are calm old colors: deep red #C8262C and dark gold #E0AB36 are used for the proscenium and curtains (gold cannot be bright yellow), blue #2F5A9A is used for the costumes; the scenery, sky, water and skin color are made of gray brown, gray and cream cardboard #F4ECD6. Leave a white scissor border for each character and scene (approximately 1/200 of the screen width). Landscapes are parallel 2.5D layers with parallax between layers and no perspective within layers. The candlelight footlight casts the paper-cut shadow about one and a half times larger on the background scene, with soft edges and flickering with the candlelight. The stage is framed by a gilded proscenium, red curtains and painted boxes of spectators, and the proscenium takes up 80 to 90% of the screen width. Camera: slow forward, panning, close-up micro-tremors; at the end, it zooms out to reveal a dark room. Avoid: bright vector colors, true 3D depth, soft photographic lighting, people with no clipped edges.

English:

Victorian toy paper theatre (Pollock's), all printed card: black lithographic outlines about 1/400 of frame width, sparse crayon hatching on one side of each piece; muted antique hand colouring: deep red #C8262C and dull gold #E0AB36 on proscenium and curtains (never bright yellow), blue #2F5A9A for costumes; scenery, sky, water and skin in greyed browns, greys and cream card #F4ECD6. Every cut-out keeps a white scissor margin (1/200 of frame width). Flats are parallel 2.5D layers in parallax, flat within each plane. Candle footlights throw each cut-out's shadow onto the scenery behind, about 1.5× enlarged, soft, flickering. Gilt proscenium, red drapes and painted audience boxes frame a stage opening 80–90% of frame width. Camera: slow push, lateral tracking, shaky close-ups; a final pull-back shows the dark room. Avoid: bright vector colour, true 3D depth, soft studio light, figures without margins.
