## Style prompt words

Opaque gouache after Welsh painter Kyffin Williams. The width of directional brush strokes is 1/80-1/40 of the width of the painting, and the length is 2-5 times the width. The edges are rough and completely covered; houses, walls, and ridges are outlined with intermittent dark short lines; a few strokes of bright green or gray-blue are scraped on the dark mountains. Color palette: sky and light light gold #F1C565 (not mustard yellow), field green #86A03F, dark coal black #1D1B1E, distant mountains and water gray blue #48505E, small dotted yellow #F6CF2F, other colors are brought out by these colors; the protagonist around whom the story revolves uses #D97757, which occupies at least one-eighth of the height of the screen. The fields and sky are brighter (about 60%), and the mountains and houses are darker. Warm colors should not exceed 10%, vignetting, and graininess. The default is low-angle side light in the evening, with fields receiving light and ridges having outline light. A true perspective view from a high place, with a road leading the line of sight down; small houses (or walls, or stones) are lined up along the hillside. Make it move: smoke or clouds are floating in circles, follow the shot horizontally. Avoid: watercolor transparency, vector flat painting, dark and dirty colors.

English:

Opaque gouache after Welsh painter Kyffin Williams. Directional strokes 1/80-1/40 of frame width, 2-5 times as long, ragged, fully covering; broken dark dashes outline buildings, walls and ridges; dark hills flecked with lighter green or slate knife marks. Palette: sky and light pale gold #F1C565 (not mustard), fields green #86A03F, darks coal #1D1B1E, far hills and water slate #48505E, small accents yellow #F6CF2F, other colours mixed from these; the hero the story centres on #D97757, at least 1/8 of frame height. Fields and sky light (~60%), hills and buildings dark. Warm grade under 10%, vignette, grain. Default low late-afternoon side light: fields lit, ridges rim-lit. From high ground in true perspective, a path leading the eye down; small repeated buildings (or walls, rocks) stepping along slopes. Motion: looping smoke or cloud, a lateral track. Avoid: watercolour transparency, vector fills, mud.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays teenage Hugh: tweed flat cap, knotted red scarf, daffodils. First, sit on the top of the mountain and look at the valley, and then walk down the street with the singing miners. Two hard cuts: Valley → Derrick Wheel → Street.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Aerial view of the daffodils on the top of the mountain: Clawd, wearing a flat hat and a red scarf, is sitting in the left foreground, holding a sprig of daffodils. The street of terraced houses leads all the way to the derricks and cinder hills at the bottom of the valley; slow pull. (1s)
2. Close-up of the derrick and the rotating wheel, with row houses crawling on the green slope behind; fixed camera position. (0.5s)
3. Horizontal follow-up shot along the street of terraced houses: the miners were walking down in three rows after work, with soot on their faces, singing as they walked; Clawd was walking in the front row, and the houses were sliding past behind him. (1s)
