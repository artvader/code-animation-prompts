English:

Black-and-white film look: cold neutral greys, a strong S-curve (blacks near #111, whites near #EEE), highlights haloing in haze, a soft old lens, silver grain re-rolled every frame (±6%), slight flicker, rare dust, vignette. Everything is grey except the one hero the story centres on, saturated, mainly clothing (e.g. terracotta #D97757); optionally one red story object (#A3241F–#C9463C). Lamps and fires stay grey, unless a flame turning from grey to warm is the story beat. Long-lens compression, soft foreground branches; in a crowd, no two people alike in build, tone or gait. Default: overcast mid-key, shadows open. Signature shot: high long-lens view of many small walking figures, the coloured one among them; without a crowd, the hero tiny in wide grey space. Reference: cinematographer Janusz Kamiński (*Schindler's List*, 1993). Avoid: sepia or blue toning; grain-free greys; more than two coloured things.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays Schindler: gray fedora, holding the reins, sitting on the horse looking down. Four cuts follow his gaze: the hillside, the telephoto subjective shot, the street, and finally ending on the candle. Each shot takes about one and a half shots. Restraint throughout: people are just walking, without any violence.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Clawd (Schindler) rides a dark horse on a hillside. The streets below are crowded with people being driven away. There is a red dot in the crowd; the camera nudges. (0.7s)
2. Cut to Schindler's telephoto subjective shot, following the little girl in the red coat walking alone in the crowd. (0.6s)
3. Cut to the street: Panning the camera at the same speed as the little girl, three rows of people are walking left from the store, and a out-of-focus coat passes in front of the camera. (0.6s)
4. Two Sabbath candles cut hard into the darkness: the flames first turn gray and then into color; slowly advancing. Clawd is out of the picture. (0.6s)
