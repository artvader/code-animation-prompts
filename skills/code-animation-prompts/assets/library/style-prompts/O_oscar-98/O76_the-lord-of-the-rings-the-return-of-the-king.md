English:

Fantasy concept-art watercolour and pencil on warm paper. Transparent washes, granulated (pigment speckle, ±6% brightness), a few blooms, darker pooled edges; every contour and ridge carries a dark-brown pencil line (about 1/800 of frame width), short hatching on shadow sides. Aerial perspective: 3–5 receding planes, each paler and cooler. Light is the subject: one strong source per scene (sun, fire, lamp, molten glow) burning through haze, its ember glow on about a quarter of the frame, about half the frame in deep umber and violet shadow. Palette: parchment #E9DCC0, deep umber #3A2418, ember orange #E07A2E, hot red #B23A1E only at the light's core, lavender #B8A8C8 for distance. Journeys may cut to a Tolkien-style map, never the main look. Camera: long lateral pans, slow push-ins to the hero. References: Alan Lee, John Howe. Avoid: smooth digital gradients; vector shapes; pale frames.

### Director’s Note

Clawd as Frodo: grey-green cloak collar, Lorien leaf brooch, Lord of the Rings on a chain. As the key film of this decade, it was given 12 shots: the map explained the entire road, Beacon took the camera and swept the mountains, the eyes were a short threat, and finally raised the Lord of the Rings to stop it. There are hard cuts between shots and long movements within each shot.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Looking down at the parchment map: The camera advances through the entire map to the Shire, and then flies along the red dotted line to Mordor. Clawd is as small as a chess piece, walking along the route. (1.3s)
2. Panorama: At dusk, the snow-capped mountains float on the sea of clouds. The nearby beacons are lit first. The camera slowly pans to the right and slightly pulls back. The beacons on the ridges light up one after another. (1.9s)
3. Close shot: The Eye of Sauron on the top of the Tower of Barad-dur. The camera zooms in and it turns to sweep out the beam. No Clawd. (0.4s)
4. The edge of the fissure of Mount Doom: The camera zooms in from a panoramic view of the basalt tongue above the lava to Clawd, with the volcano and eyes behind it, lifting the glowing Ring from the edge of the abyss and holding it high. (1.5s)
