The written words are formulas, numbers or notes related to the subject matter. The small blocks of writing only occupy about a quarter of the glass. Variation: Dark green slate chalk with afterimage. Color: Slate Green #2F3E35, Chalk White #F1EFE6, Light Amber #E0A050, Soft Gold #D9B45A. The camera slowly advances, getting closer to the words every time it switches. Benchmarking photographer Roger Deakins (A Beautiful Mind, 2001). English:

Handwriting as the image: translucent white wax-marker strokes (about 1/300 of frame width) appear stroke by stroke on a window of square leaded panes: formulas, figures or notes about the subject, in small blocks on a quarter of the glass. Outside, the subject's world, soft-focus, mid-toned and warm (overcast gold, muted green), never brighter than the writing. A warm interior (shelves, green-shaded lamp, amber light) frames the window. At the key moment, chosen symbols brighten and soft gold lines join them into a star or polygon, lights running along. Variant: chalk on green slate with ghost writing. Palette: slate green #2F3E35, chalk white #F1EFE6, lamp amber #E0A050, soft gold #D9B45A. Camera: slow push-ins, each cut closer to the writing. Reference: cinematographer Roger Deakins (*A Beautiful Mind*, 2001). Avoid: HUD graphics; typed fonts; writing filling the glass; cold blue outside.

## Replenish

### Technique keywords

White oil pen on glass, blackboard chalk, glowing golden connecting lines, Deakins style window light

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays Nash: open shirt collar, loose knit tie, a grease pen in front of the window. The first shot is writing, the next two shots are light; each hard cut is closer to the numbers, and in the end Clawd paints, leaving only the pattern.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Medium panorama: In front of the Gothic library window, Clawd pretends to be Nash and stands on the window seat, using a white oil-based pen to write game theory formulas on the glass. Outside the window is the courtyard in autumn; the camera slows down. (1s)
2. Close shot: the writing on the glass, out of focus outside the window, Clawd’s pen is exposed in the lower left corner; the characters light up, and the golden thread connects them into a star; the camera is slightly zoomed out. (0.8s)
3. Close shot: A group of five password numbers on the blackboard. The numbers light up one by one, and the golden seven-pointed star pattern is drawn by itself; the camera is slightly zoomed out. No Clawd. (0.8s)
