## Style prompt words

Night oil painting portrait. On the smooth solid volume, redraw with two short strokes along the shape, with small contrast (brightness ±8%): for the sea, sky, and clothes, use a wide brush, about 1/60 of the width of the picture; for the face, use a thin brush of 1/150, keeping it smooth. Separate colors according to purpose: medium light blue-violet #32377a is used for the sky, sea, and distant land, accounting for about 60% of the picture; the surface exposed to the moonlight and the sand are brightened to light lavender blue; green #128a91 is used in the water and the light-receiving side; magenta #7a2f86 is used for the outline light; and #07071c is used for the deepest dark part. The top surface of any skin tone is affected by blue light, with wet highlights, and there is light when the eyes are open. By default, at night, the only main light is the full moon, and the water surface is covered with broken light; the magenta is just the soft outline light of the distant light source; other small light sources are just a small bit of cyan light. Pellets ±3%. Signature shot: The character is calm, occupying about two-thirds of the height of the painting. Push slowly. Reference to Barry Jenkins' "Moonlight" (2016). Avoid: thick brush strokes, small black eyes in plaster, and dull near-black images.

English:

Night oil portraiture: smooth volumes repainted in two passes of short, low-contrast (±8% value) form-following strokes: broad (1/60 of frame width) on water, sky and cloth, fine (1/150) on faces, which stay smooth. Palette by role: sky, sea and far land mid blue-violet #32377a, about 60% of the frame; moonlit planes and sand pale lavender-blue; teal #128a91 in water and lit sides; magenta #7a2f86 rim; deepest shadows #07071c. Any skin takes blue top planes and a wet highlight, eyes open and lit. Default night: one key, a full moon with a broken glitter path on water; magenta only as a soft rim from a distant glow; other small light sources stay cyan-white points. Grain ±3%. Signature shot: the figure calm, ~2/3 of frame height. Slow push-ins. Reference: Barry Jenkins, *Moonlight* (2016). Avoid: coarse dabs, small dark statue eyes, a murky frame.

Clawd plays little Chiron without a costume: first being held in the water during swimming lessons, then standing between three faces, and finally sitting on the beach next to Kevin. 3. Cut to the beach under the moonlight: a double shot of Kevin and Clawd sitting side by side on the blue beach, with the full moon on the upper right and waves lapping on the shore; the camera slows down. (0.9s)
