It follows the shape. The cross lines are only used in the dark parts. The outline on the backlit side is thickened, and light ink is used in the distance. Warm black #17130F printed on newsprint #E8E0CC; the main character around whom the story revolves is a separate accent color plate (like #D97757), overprinted 1–2 pixels off. English:

1890s newspaper print. Set text as a sharp mask, then print: ~1 px blur and a noisy threshold for ink spread, ragged edges, under-inked patches, faint mirrored show-through. Page grammar: masthead, a wood-type headline across the width (~1/6 of frame height), 3-6 columns of small type in English unless another language is explicitly requested (~1/80 of frame height) flanking one large engraved picture. Wood-type letters are grained blocks. Everything pictured is a line engraving: hatching lines about 1/1000 of frame width, spaced 1/400, following the form, cross-hatching only in darks, heavier shadow-side contours, paler distance. Warm black #17130F on newsprint #E8E0CC; the hero the story centres on gets one accent plate (e.g. #D97757), offset 1-2 px. Motion: type blocks slam down, bounce and leave their print; one repeating mechanical motion, pages turning. Avoid: clean digital type, smooth grey shading, other colours.
