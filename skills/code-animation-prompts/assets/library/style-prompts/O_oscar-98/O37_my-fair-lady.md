## Style prompt words

A 60s fashion illustration inspired by Bob Peak. The ink line is very fast: it becomes thicker at the corners, thinner at the straight lines, and the back part becomes white when the pen is closed; the thickness is between 1/600 and 1/250 of the width of the picture, and becomes thinner at the small bends. Use a solid ink block for dark clothes, hats, and stripes; leave the face white on paper #FBF7F1. Watercolor is a loose and soft-edged color pool, and the edges are darker after drying: pink #EE84B2 as the main color, magenta #CE3076 as the hot spot, plus rose pink #F4B2CC and a bit of plum #80285C; use warm gray for secondary characters and shadows; use directional throw points. The scene was painted with just a few threads and these kinds of watercolors. The horizontal figures are the core: slender sideways, nine to ten heads high, repeated horizontally (the protagonist plus a warm gray figure or a double shadow), black and white patterns pressed on pink; watercolor covers 60 to 80% of the picture. Lens: Move slowly along the characters; use graphic masks for transitions. Avoid: outlines with the same thickness, flat digital colors, polygonal hard-edged color blocks, and a lonely person on a blank page.

English:

1960s fashion illustration after Bob Peak. Fast ink line, thicker in turns, dry-brush tail, a flick off; 1/600–1/250 of frame width, thinning on tight curves. Solid black for dark clothing, hats and stripes; faces bare paper #FBF7F1. Loose, soft-edged watercolour pools, darker at drying edges: pink #EE84B2 as the main field, magenta #CE3076 for hot spots, rose #F4B2CC, a touch of plum #80285C; warm grey for secondary figures and shading; directional splatter. Settings are a few lines in these washes, not literal colour. The frieze is essential: slender profile figures 9–10 heads tall repeated across the frame (the hero plus grey-wash figures or echoes), black-and-white pattern over pink; washes cover 60–80% of the frame. Camera: slow drift along the figures; graphic-mask transitions. Avoid: even-width outlines, flat digital fills, polygon-edged washes, a lone figure on an empty page.
