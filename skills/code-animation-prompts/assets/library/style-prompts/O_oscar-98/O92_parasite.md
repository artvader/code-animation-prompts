### Director’s Note

Clawd plays the role of Ki-woo, and he keeps the landscape stone in his hand all the way: holding it above his head on the lawn, hiding by the coffee table on a rainy night, and carrying it all the way down the mountain. There are only two cuts in the entire section; in each shot, the camera moves up and down the section, allowing the rich and the poor to be in the same frame, and the 12 shots finally stop on the entire section.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Section of the glass mansion and party on the striped lawn during the day: Clawd stands at the garden door, holding the landscape stone above his head with both hands; the camera slowly zooms in. (0.8s)
2. Cut to a rainy night: Clawd is hiding next to the coffee table in the Pu family's living room; the camera goes down like an elevator, through the floor and basement, and falls to the bunker, with the lights flashing at random. (1.3s)
3. Cut to a long shot going down in the rain: the camera follows Clawd, with the landscape stone on his head, walking down the long steps from the gate, passing through the tunnel under the road, entering the lower alleys and the semi-basement that is being flooded, and then rises and expands into a full cross-section, with the mansion at the top and the flooded bottom. (2.9s)
