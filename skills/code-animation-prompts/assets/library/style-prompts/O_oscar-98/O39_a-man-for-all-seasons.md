## Style prompt words

After Northern Renaissance oil portrait by Hans Holbein the Younger. Painting layer by layer: colored base, smooth light and dark brushstrokes, soft cover dyeing, and finally fine brushstrokes; the finished product is smooth, with faint brushstrokes visible on the background and fabric. Each material has its own painting method: fur is painted one by one, velvet has bright pleats and sharp ridges, metal has two highlight points on each piece, and woolen materials and leather have soft suede surfaces. Each subject is painted as a formal bust wearing his own clothes; its scene is revealed from a window, or is indicated by an object in the hand. Swatches: Dark Green Curtain #1F4A26, Black #141113, Velvet Red #6E0C15, Gold #B0801E, Fur Brown #3E2818; warm skin, full volume. Soft light comes from the upper left. Signature shot: a bust against a green curtain; followed by a close-up of the material, and then slowly zooming out to a dark room illuminated by a pool of warm light. Finish with a layer of warm varnish and light crackling. Avoid: Flat paint, digital hard edges, even shine, and a face with no volume.

English:

Oil portrait after Hans Holbein the Younger. Paint in layers: a toned underlayer, directional strokes following light and shadow, soft glazes, fine detail last; smooth finish, brushwork faint in background and fabric. Render each material: fur hair by hair, velvet folds with sharp light ridges, metal with two highlights per piece, wool and leather with soft nap. Any subject becomes a formal half-length sitter in its own clothes; its setting shows through a window or in an object it holds. Palette: deep green curtain #1F4A26, black #141113, velvet red #6E0C15, gold #B0801E, fur brown #3E2818; warm, fully modelled skin. Soft light from upper left. Signature shot: the portrait against the curtain; then material close-ups, a slow pull back into a dark room lit by warm pools. Warm varnish, light craquelure. Avoid: flat fills, hard digital edges, even light, unmodelled faces.
