## Style prompt words

1939 impasto oil painting in Technicolor. Squeegee color block: Use long horizontal strips of color for the sky (length is 1/15–1/6 of the width of the painting, and thickness is about 1/60). Use short, conformal strokes on the ground and characters, leaving calm, thin areas in between; the paint edges are affected by the light from the upper left, and become brighter and thicker, with cloth lines exposed in thin areas. The default is the sunset sky, from burgundy #3A0912 through bright red #A8241A, orange #F27A22 and burnt to gold #FFD878; the ground is reddish brown #6E2214; the silhouette is near black with warmth; the protagonist around the story maintains a clearly visible warm midtone (such as #D97757) and does not make a silhouette. Strong backlight, golden outline light; add a warm side light to illuminate half of the face and hands in the close shot. The composition is like an epic poster: large dark silhouettes (trees, houses, rocks, people) pressed against a low horizon. Lamp flames painted with oil. Camera movement: The panoramic view is slowly advanced, the camera is pushed closer to the protagonist from a low position, and finally the crane is raised to see the big scene. Avoid: same-sized pieces all over the screen, dirty browns, smooth gradients.

English:

Impasto oil in a 1939 Technicolor palette. Palette-knife slabs: long horizontal sky bands (1/15-1/6 of frame width long, about 1/60 tall), shorter strokes following form on land and figures, calm thin areas between; ridges lit from upper left, paint thickest in highlights, canvas weave where thin. Default sunset sky: wine #3A0912, red #A8241A, orange #F27A22, gold #FFD878; ground red-brown #6E2214; warm near-black silhouettes. The hero the story centres on stays a readable warm mid-tone (e.g. #D97757), never a silhouette. Strong backlight, gold rims; in close shots a warm side light on half the face and hands. Epic-poster composition: big dark silhouettes (trees, buildings, rocks, figures) on a low horizon. Lamps as painted flames. Camera: slow push on the wide, low push-in on the hero, crane up to a vista. Avoid: tiled same-size strokes, muddy browns, smooth gradients.

Clawd plays Scarlett: a wide-brimmed straw hat with a green ribbon and a carrot in his hand. The sky burns from gold to blood red in the first 7 shots, and the carrot is lifted in the 6th shot.
