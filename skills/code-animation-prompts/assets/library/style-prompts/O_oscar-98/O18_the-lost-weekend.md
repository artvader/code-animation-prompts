## Style prompt words

The width of the grid is about 1/150 of the frame width and the height of the grid is twice the width of the grid. 85%–95% of the grid is typed, leaving only the brightest light blank. The gray scale is graded by word keys (light . : -, medium = + o, dark The form is read through clearly defined letter blocks and silhouettes. Each material repeats its own key (e.g. vertical structure I H, brick joint _|, water ~). Signs or instructions should be typed so that they can be read. The ink color of the ribbon is uneven: half of the ribbon is missing ink, and several keys are always on the high or low side. English:

Type every image: one bold monospaced glyph per keystroke, fixed grid, cells 1/150 of frame width wide, twice as tall. Strike 85-95% of cells; bare paper only for highlights. Grey from a key ladder (light . : -, mid = + o, dark X # M); darks (about 30%) are overstrikes like M#. Shapes read as crisp-edged fields, clear silhouettes. Each material repeats its own keys (e.g. I H verticals, _| courses, ~ water). Signs stay legible. Uneven ribbon: half-inked strikes, a few keys always high or low. Paper #EBE6DA, ink #1D1C21; only the hero the story centres on, at least 1/3 of frame height, is struck in red-orange #D4704F, its shadow black. Hard sidelight casts long, crisp, dense shadows. The picture types itself within the first second, then holds. Reference: Paul Smith's typewriter art. Avoid: sparse frames, shapeless streaks.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays Don Bonnam: the character wears a fedora hat, carries a typewriter that he wants to pawn, and walks on four legs alternately frame by frame. The street appears around it as if it were typed out by a typewriter; then two quick hard cuts, a close shot and a CLOSED sign, complete the explanation of "the pawn shop is closed". Performs at 1.8x speed.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. One-point perspective panorama under the Third Avenue Elevated Railway: The street scene is printed line by line on the paper. Clawd, wearing a fedora hat, is walking forward with a typewriter; the camera pulls back slightly. (1.3s)
2. Close shot of Clawd: the character’s hat, eyes and gray typewriter beside him, with steel beams behind; the camera slows down. (0.6s)
3. Close-up of the signboard of J. GOLDMAN's LOANS pawn shop. The letters are made up of characters, and the CLOSED sign hangs below; the camera slows down. (0.6s)
