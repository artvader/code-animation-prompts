A modern homage to late-1920s silent film in black and white. Clean tones: glowing highlights, soft mid-greys, deep blacks; a gentle S-curve, grey halation around lights, slight flicker (±3%), fine grain, rare dust, a 1.33 pillarbox inside 16:9. Intertitles on black with an Art Deco border and spaced capitals, in English unless another language is explicitly requested. Camera: smooth push-ins and pull-backs, title cards flashed in, cuts landing on gestures (a bow, a wave). Reference: cinematographer Guillaume Schiffman (*The Artist*, 2011). ### Director’s Note

Clawd plays George Valentine: white wing collar, black bow tie, silk top hat, cane, and Uggie, a Jack Russell terrier. Five fast cuts, close to real speed: hat raised on the stairs, BANG! The subtitle card flashed, the puppy fell to the ground, and finally the audience stood up and bowed. Clawd is the only color.
