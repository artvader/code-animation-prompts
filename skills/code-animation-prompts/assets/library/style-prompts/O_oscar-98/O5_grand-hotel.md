### Director’s Note

Clawd plays the role of a doorman, wearing a round hat and carrying a suitcase covered with hotel labels. It's really walking: 1.4 meters, 2.4 steps per second, and the steps match the displacement. The entire segment is played in 1:1, and it cuts from an overhead shot to a side tracking shot at 1.08 seconds.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Looking straight down from under the skylight: the six-story cloister circles toward the key tower. The orange doorman is the only dot on the checkerboard marble, walking across the ground; the camera slowly zooms in. (1.1s)
2. Follow Clawd from the side according to his height: he is walking forward carrying a suitcase, the lobby slides behind him, and the revolving door in front is rotating; the camera moves forward and inward while following. (1.4s)
