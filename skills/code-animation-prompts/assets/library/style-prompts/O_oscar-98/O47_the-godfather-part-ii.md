## Style prompt words

Hand-colored sepia silver print, circa 1900. First make a three-dimensional shape that receives light. The soft light comes from the upper left, and then "print" it out: soft focus with a slight halo, and the grain is the heaviest in the middle (±5%); the sepia tone is completely spread from #281A12 through #987048 to #EEE0C2, and at least one-fifth of the frame falls to the deepest end, which is the same under any light. The proportions of the characters are realistic (the head is about one-seventh of the height), and the light and shade are soft. The transparent paint is applied loosely and spills over the edges: the only main character is the heaviest (for example, orange-red #D97757, opacity 60 to 80%), and one or two props are lightly applied; the color is swallowed up in the dark areas. The distressing should be light: dark corners, silvering on the edges (the dark parts on the edges have a grayish-blue metallic sheen), a few small mildew spots, fine scratches, and at most one cracked corner. The composition is frontal and documentary, with the characters standing quietly facing the camera. The camera pans slowly across faces. Compare Lewis Hine and Augustus Sherman's Ellis Island photos. Avoid: building block characters, digital coloring, low-contrast fog, and large mold spots.

English:

A hand-coloured toned silver print from about 1900. Build lit 3D forms, soft key from upper left, then 'print' them: soft focus, light halation, grain heaviest in mid-tones (±5%), full sepia range #281A12, #987048, #EEE0C2; at least a fifth of the frame reaches the dark end, in any light. Figures: real proportions (head 1/7 of body), soft shading. Transparent dyes brushed loosely past edges: the one hero subject strongly (e.g. orange-red #D97757 at 60–80% opacity), a faint touch on one or two props; dyes die in shadow. Light ageing: vignette, silvered edges (grey-blue sheen in border darks), small foxing spots, fine scratches, at most one cracked corner. Frontal, documentary: figures stand still facing the lens. Camera: slow pan across faces. References: Lewis Hine; Augustus Sherman (Ellis Island). Avoid: toy figures, digital fill colour, low-contrast fog, big mould blotches.
