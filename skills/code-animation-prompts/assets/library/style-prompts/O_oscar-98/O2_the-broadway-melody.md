### Director’s Note

Clawd is a tap dancer with a straw hat and a black cane who taps from start to finish. The opening fades in from the white space of Part 1; on Beat 4 the microphone comes alive and sound waves pour out (talkies are coming). Four hard cuts: street corner → stage → microphone → song and dance group → Clawd.
