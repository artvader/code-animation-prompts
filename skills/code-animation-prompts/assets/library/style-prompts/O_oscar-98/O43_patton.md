1940s US war-bond lithograph poster. Flat inks: red #C2342C, navy #213261, olive #5F5C31, khaki #B7975E, on fibrous, foxed cream #E6D5B3 paper. ### Director’s Note

Clawd as Patton: four-star steel helmet, orders and medals, ivory-handled revolver, riding crop. It walks into place under the stripes, snaps and salutes as it cuts into a close shot, stops, puts it down, slaps the riding crop into the palm of the hand, and nods, like the beginning of a lecture. Two hard cuts, and the final zoom makes it shrink to a small dot in front of the giant flag.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Red and white stripes cover the screen (continued from a graphic match of Warhol’s grid), and then the camera zooms out, and Clawd walks up from the bottom of the screen and stands in front of the huge national flag. (1s)

3. Panorama: The camera pulls out and looks beyond the flag to see the entire empty theater: red curtain, gilded proscenium, footlights, Clawd shrinks to a small dot in the center of the stage. (0.5s)
