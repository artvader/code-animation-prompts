## Style prompt words

Late 19th century circus chromolithograph, after the Strobridge Lithograph Company. Flat ink and thick black outline (approximately 1/300 of the frame width); shadows are crescent-shaped and deepened, and lines are added; light comes from the upper left. Four lithographs: chrome yellow #F4C416, vermilion #D62C24, cobalt blue #1E4EA0, black #181210, printed on warm cream paper #EBD9B4; overprinting and light mesh yield only orange, green, brown, khaki, light blue, grey. Solid printing, only the gradient areas such as the sky, skin, and ground are hung with a screen (dots are about 1/250 of the width of the frame). The screen angles of each version are different, misaligned by 1-3 pixels, and have lithographic grains. The story revolves around a protagonist who stacks yellow solids on vermilion solids to get a flat orange (like #D97757). The composition uses exaggerated perspective: radiating lines converge towards the main action, and several things happen at the same time; when there is a crowd, it is painted into many identifiable little people, and even only one character is established. Camera: pan up, pan, cut to climax in the action. Avoid: Colors other than the four colors, people with only polka dots, protagonists hanging from nets in pink.

English:

A circus chromolithograph after the Strobridge Lithographing Co. (c. 1890). Flat ink, heavy black outlines (about 1/300 of frame width), crescent shadows, light from upper left. Four stones: chrome yellow #F4C416, vermilion #D62C24, cobalt #1E4EA0, key black #181210 on warm cream paper #EBD9B4; overprints and tints give only orange, green, brown, tan, pale blue, grey. Solids print flat; screens (dots about 1/250 of frame width) only on gradients (sky, skin, ground), each stone at its own angle, 1-3 px off register, stone grain. The hero the story centres on is solid yellow under solid vermilion, a flat orange (e.g. #D97757). Exaggerated perspective, radial lines converging on the action, several actions at once; any crowd is tiny individual figures, though a lone figure works. Camera: tilt up, lateral dolly, cut on action. Avoid: off-palette colours, dot-only crowds, a screened, pinkish hero.

### Director’s Note

Clawd as Sebastian the Great: a sequined cape trimmed with gold scallops, hung upside down on a swing. Swing up to find it on the swing bar. The elephant in the middle moves sideways to keep the parade moving forward. Finally, the action cuts to the moment when people are picked up. The time is stuck when the swing swings to the right, consistent with the composition of the poster. It swings its arms towards the audience.
