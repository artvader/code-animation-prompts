## Style prompt words

Edward Hopper-style American realistic painting. Large blocks of restrained, light plane colors: institutional green #9EB8A0 and #5E7C66, gray-green ground #7E8876, cream #ECE6D2, visible canvas grain (±3%), drag several long strokes on each block, about 1/80 of the width of the painting, five to ten percent brighter or darker. Low-angle sunlight, coming from one side, casts a hard-edged warm parallelogram #FCE6AC on walls and floors, pressing clear shadows against window frames or railings, both indoors and out. The sky is light cream green, the skin is a warm cream color, and the clothes are a dark green. The characters are made up of three or four stacked blocks of light and dark colors with soft edges, no outlines, and realistic proportions; when there are several people, they stand separately. Most of the time it's still, and then something happens in less than a second: a flick of the camera or a burst of bright light. Signature shot: a still room in one-point perspective, with patches of light on the ground. Compare Hopper's "Office in a Small City" (1953). Avoid: thick outlines, vector people, deep, saturated walls, blurry flares.

English:

Edward Hopper-style American realist painting. Large flat planes of restrained, pale colour: institutional greens #9EB8A0 and #5E7C66, grey-green floor #7E8876, cream #ECE6D2, on visible canvas (±3%), with a few long dragged strokes about 1/80 of frame width, 5–10% off the base. Low side sun throws hard-edged parallelograms of warm light #FCE6AC on walls and ground, crossed by crisp shadows of frames or railings, indoors or out. Sky pale cream-green; skin warmed cream; clothes darkened greens. Figures painted from 3–4 overlapping soft-edged value shapes, no outline, real proportions; several people stand apart. Stillness, then one sudden event under a second: a whip pan, a burst of light. Signature shot: a still one-point interior, light on the floor. References: Edward Hopper, *Office in a Small City* (1953). Avoid: heavy outlines, vector figures, dark saturated walls, fuzzy light patches.
