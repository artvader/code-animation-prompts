## Style prompt words

Linen postcards from the 1950s and 1960s, from Curt Teich Press. Plain weave cardboard: The ink only falls on the raised warp and weft, forming a fine checkered pattern about 1/300 of the width of the picture, with white spots exposed in the recesses. Hand-separated saturated colors, airbrushed with soft transitions: sky blue #3fa0dc, grass green #4fae57, pink #f08cb0, use turquoise #2fb5b8 for the main object or water surface, and cream cardboard #f6e4b8 as the background color and white edge. The main object should be large, front and side, accounting for about half the height of the card. Glossy surface with four-pointed star glitter; color misalignment by 1–2 pixels. Large three-dimensional navy blue characters with white outlines are placed horizontally on one-third of the card. Write the place name or film title (if not, compile one according to the theme). Each letter is embedded with a small scene, with a red ribbon on top. The opening postcard is placed on the table. After it is pushed to fill the screen, the picture comes alive (water is moving, people are walking), and then moves sideways. Avoid: Gradient without texture, gray colors, photo perspective.

English:

A 1950s–60s linen postcard, Curt Teich manner. Linen-weave card: ink only on raised threads, a fine crosshatch about 1/300 of frame width, white specks in the hollows. Saturated hand-separated colour, airbrush blends: sky blue #3fa0dc, grass green #4fae57, pink #f08cb0, teal #2fb5b8 for the main object or water, cream card #f6e4b8 for paper and border. The main object large, side-on, about half the card's height. Star glints on shiny surfaces; registration off 1–2 px. Big navy 3D block letters with white outlines across the top third spell the place or title (invent one if needed), a small scene in each letter, a red ribbon banner above. Open on the card on a desk, push in until it fills the frame and comes alive (water, figures move), then track sideways. Avoid: weave-less gradients, greyish colour, photographic perspective.
