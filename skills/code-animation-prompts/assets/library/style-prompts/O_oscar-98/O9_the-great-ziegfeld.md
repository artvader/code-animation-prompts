### Director’s Note

Clawd is the star of the cake: a rhinestone headband with a tuft of ostrich feathers tumbling down like a fountain. The scene was run at 1.2x; the star didn't show his face until the last cut, and in shot 5 he turned around, jumped, and threw his arms into a V. Two hard cuts connect the curtain, boom and top floor.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Frontal low camera: The chiffon curtain flies up from the rotating cake stage, revealing layers of girls holding fans, with a small Clawd on top; the camera tilts up. (0.9s)
2. Climb up the spiral boom, passing the feather fan, white grand piano and violinist, and stop just below the top floor. (0.8s)
3. Almost eye-level medium shot: Clawd on top, wearing an ostrich feather headdress, turns to face the camera, swinging his arms into a V shape; slow push. (0.8s)
