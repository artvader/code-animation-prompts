## Style prompt words

Moving 1880s gold-toned albumen photo. First, draw a flat shape with soft edges, divided according to brightness: the main body is deep (15%–30%), the ground is 50%–60%, and the sky is about 90%; there are visible lines on the object surface and grass (the line width is about 1/300 of the frame width). Then "print": convert to grayscale, soft focus (approximately 1/800 of the frame width), three layers of horizontally staggered light shadows are stretched with the lens, multiplied by cream color #F2DCB4, black level raised to warm brown #2E1C0F, uneven development (±5%), vignetting pressed to #785634, grain refreshed in each frame (±4%), sparse dust scratches. Just hand-color (like #D97757) the main character around whom the story revolves, like a clear dye, with a mask feathering of 2–3 pixels; the lighting is still sepia. Horizontal panorama, with a strip in the middle lined with repeated small elements: people, animals, vehicles. If the subject matter is deserted, use fences, wooden piles, and trees; don't let a whole large area occupy the picture. The sky is white by default. Camera movement: Slow out from a close-up shot of the main character to a full shot for 4-5 seconds. Avoid: hard ink lines, colors other than the main character, clean digital skies, gray prints.

English:

A moving 1880s gold-toned albumen photograph. Paint soft-edged flat shapes by brightness: main subjects dark (15-30%), ground 50-60%, sky about 90%; visible hatching and grass tufts (lines ~1/300 of frame width). Then print: grayscale, slight blur (~1/800 of frame width), a faint smear of three horizontal offset copies growing with camera speed, multiply cream #F2DCB4, lift blacks to umber #2E1C0F, blotchy development (±5%), vignette to #785634, per-frame grain (±4%), dust, scratches. Hand-tint only the hero the story centres on (e.g. #D97757) as transparent dye, mask feathered 2-3 px; lamps stay sepia. Wide panorama: the middle band packed with repeated small elements (figures, animals, vehicles; for a lone subject fences, posts, trees), no big plane dominating. Camera: one 4-5 s ease-out pull back from the hero to the panorama. Avoid: hard ink outlines, colour beyond the hero, a flat grey print.
