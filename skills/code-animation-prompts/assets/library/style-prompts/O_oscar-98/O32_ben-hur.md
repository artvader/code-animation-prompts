English:

1959 65mm widescreen epic built in 3D. Default: hard high sun (about 50°), short crisp shadows, cobalt sky fading to sandy white; under a low sun shadows stay hard but long, the sky turns amber. Marble, gilt, red granite and sandstone, with mouldings and fluted columns, never bare boxes. Warm sunlit haze mixes about 20% into mid-distance colours. Technicolor-print grade: warm, saturated, slightly contrasty, no clipping or bloom; fine grain (±3%), slight gate weave. Scale is the point: monumental architecture, statues and repeated elements fill the background, never empty. The one hero subject (e.g. orange #D97757) is the most saturated thing in frame; any repeated motion (stride, wheel, oar) keeps time with speed. Signature shot: low tracking beside the moving hero, 35–50 mm full-frame; then wide, top-down, close-up, low angle. Avoid: clean CG light, empty or primitive sets, overexposure.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays Judas Ben-Hur: a linen headband on his forehead, a rein in one hand and a whip in the other, driving four white horses. Five seconds and six shots, follow the shooting method of the second film crew in 1959: starting, turning, following shots, wheels, close shots, and poster composition. Every camera position is moving with the chariot, and the rhythm is not lost.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Panoramic view of the starting white line: nine four-horse chariots are lined up side by side, with Clawd’s white horse team among them, and the stands behind them are full; the team rushes out, and the camera slowly tightens. (0.9s)
2. Aerial shot from a high position on the East Bend: The chariot is thrown around the gold-plated turning pillar, with an arc of dust rising behind it. (0.8s)
3. Follow Clawd’s chariot side by side: the white horse is running at full speed, the spokes of the chariot are flying, and dust is flying; the camera moves forward with the chariot. (1.1s)
4. Low camera between the two cars: Messala's serrated hub bites into Clawd's wheel, sparks flying; the camera follows the car. (0.6s)
5. Close shot of Clawd cracking his whip in his chariot, with Messala's black horse beside him; slow approach. (0.8s)
6. Poster composition: Low-angle side shot, Clawd's white horses are tied under the golden seated giant on the central partition wall, with the stands and the Governor's Stand behind them; the camera slowly widens. (0.8s)
