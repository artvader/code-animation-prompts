## Style prompt words

2001 newspaper color dots, viewed on a library microfilm reader. Signboard shot: The picture itself fills the screen, without headers and text columns; the lines and words (signboards, labels) in the picture should remain solid lines. The four-color dot angles are 15°, 75°, 0°, and 45°. The dots are approximately 1/250 of the screen width, and the color registration is wrong by 1 pixel. The whole is converted to a low-contrast fluorescent green monochrome, and the dots are still clearly visible: base #1b2320, frame gap #0c110f, highlights not brighter than #a0dcaf. Only the protagonist of the story retains the original color (such as #D97757), with dots and the same low contrast; the light source always turns green. A scan line every 3 pixels, a pale swath rolling across every 4 seconds, a soft light spot with gray and a hair on the glass, a rounded hood that only lightly presses about 5% of the outer ring, and a line of small constant-width film numbers. Field change slides the film horizontally across the dark frame gap (0.5 seconds); slow forward within the shot. Avoid: newspaper layout, color leakage, hard cutting.

English:

A 2001 newspaper colour halftone on a library microfiche reader. Signature shot: the picture fills the frame, no headline or columns; lines and lettering inside it stay solid. CMYK screens at 15°, 75°, 0°, 45°, dots about 1/250 of frame width, 1 px misregistration. Convert to low-contrast phosphor-green monochrome, dots still clearly visible: film base #1b2320, frame gaps #0c110f, highlights no brighter than #a0dcaf. Only the story's one hero keeps its colour, whole figure (e.g. #D97757), screened and equally low-contrast; every light source turns green. Scan lines every 3 px, a faint scan bar every 4 s, a lamp hot-spot, dust, a hair, a light rounded hood vignette (outer ~5%), a small monospace fiche index. Scene changes slide the fiche sideways past a dark gap (0.5 s); slow push-ins. Avoid: newspaper layout, colour leaking, hard cuts.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays reporter on the Spotlight team: lanyard badge, shorthand notebook, highlighted pen at the back. The three frames of microfilm are connected without hard cutting, but by sliding the film horizontally in the reader, and the last shot lands on the front page.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Panoramic view of the underground newsroom: reporters gathered around a table piled with paper, with a fluorescent light grill above their heads. Clawd stood in front, hanging his work badge and holding a shorthand notebook; the camera slowly zoomed out. (0.8s)
2. The film slides horizontally to the next frame: an overhead shot of the printed list of priests. The toy-sized Clawd pushes a large highlighter to draw down line by line, and the camera moves down. (0.8s)
3. Sliding to the printing factory pier: medium and close shot, Clawd stands next to the front page, the title is "Church allowed abuse by priest for years", the camera slows down. (0.9s)
