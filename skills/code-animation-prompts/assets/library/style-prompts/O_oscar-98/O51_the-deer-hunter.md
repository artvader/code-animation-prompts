## Style prompt words

Andrew Wyeth style tempera on plaster base. Dry, matte, very fine: The colors are all produced by densely intersecting short strokes. Each stroke is about 1/400 of the width of the painting and as thin as a hair. Low saturation colors: pale cloudy #D9D5C6, grass yellow #CDB27A, ocher brown #6A5234, gray-green distant mountains #8E938C, rust color #8A4E32; water, stone, clothes and skin are all drawn from these colors. The accent color is only given to the only protagonist (such as a red hat); the light source in the painting is just a faint dot without halo. The ground (the default is dead grass, the same applies to other surfaces) is first laid out in large blocks of different shades, then thin brushes, dark grass balls, and finally single grass blades combed by the wind; rocks and houses are arranged along all sides with short brushes. The space is empty and lonely, and the subject is small and off-center. The default is soft light on cloudy days, and at other times, keep the light pale and not warm. The camera drifts slowly and zooms in quietly; the circular mask can be used as a cutting point. Avoid: Wet watercolor smudges, saturated colors, heavy shiny strokes.

English:

Egg tempera on gesso panel after Andrew Wyeth. Dry, matte, very fine: colour built from dense cross-hatched strokes, each about 1/400 of frame width long, hairline thin. Muted palette: pale overcast sky #D9D5C6, straw grass #CDB27A, umber #6A5234, misty grey-green hills #8E938C, rust #8A4E32; water, stone, clothes and skin mixed from these. One accent colour, only on the one hero subject (e.g. a red cap); any light source is a small pale dot, no halo. Ground (dry grass by default, other ground alike): big light and dark patches, fine strokes, dark tufts, then single wind-combed blades; rocks, buildings hatched along their planes. Empty, lonely space; the subject small, off-centre. Soft overcast light; at other times of day stay pale, no warm glow. Camera: slow drift, quiet push; a circular iris may mark a cut. Avoid: wet watercolour blooms, saturated colour, thick shiny strokes.

### Director’s Note

Clawd as Mike: red wool hunting hat, shotgun with scope. River Valley → Scope → Deer Eye → Hunter, four hard cuts, average speed of about 1.24 times, quiet and stable. The performance point is that the shot was not fired: put down the gun, close your eyes, exhale, and then look up at the deer.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Panoramic view of the steel town in the mist of the river valley: blast furnaces, smoking chimneys, onion-top churches; the camera pans slowly. (0.6s)
2. Scope perspective: Black circular mask and crosshairs, stag on the ridge; the picture shakes slightly with the hunter's hand. (0.7s)
3. Close-up of the stag’s head and eyes; camera zooms in slowly. (0.3s)
4. Clawd pretended to be Mike and stood on the granite, put down the shotgun, closed his eyes and exhaled, then looked up at the buck; the camera zoomed out to cover the entire ridge. (0.8s)
