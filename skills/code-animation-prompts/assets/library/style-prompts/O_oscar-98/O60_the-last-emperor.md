## Style prompt words

Silk with fine brushwork and heavy color (court painting method of Qing Dynasty). Drawing from far to near, each shape is: flat background color → divided dyeing (deepening along the lower edge and covered areas) → uniform iron line drawing (dark brown, line width is about 1/900 of the painting) → gold and whitening; finally, the whole painting is overlaid with warm ocher cover dyeing with a concentration of 30%, and the lines are still clear. Mineral colors: cinnabar #C23A22, azurite #2C5A9A, stone green #3A8A6A, garcinia #E8B830, mud gold #CAA04A. The quilt is dyed and pressed firmly; the ground and sky are warm ocher silk colors with medium lightness, not nearly white, not saturated blue sky and orange sunset, and the time period changes only depend on the overall coldness and warmth. Don't draw shadows and glare. Use fine lines for water lines. Boundary drawing is used for architecture, and the tiles are drawn one by one; when there is a group of people, everyone is different, and it can also be done by a single person. The silky lines are light (±3%) and the corners are dark brown. One-point perspective, left and right symmetry, the big man is in front, and the background is deep and orderly. Push slowly. It is compared to the long imperial scrolls of the Qing Dynasty, such as "Kangxi's Southern Inspection Map". Avoid: Copying and pasting characters; flat painting without dyeing; freehand ink; poster-style saturated colors.

English:

Chinese gongbi zhongcai (fine-line, heavy-colour court painting) on aged silk. Far to near, each form: flat base, a wash darkening its lower, overlapped edges, an even iron-wire outline (dark brown, ~1/900 frame width), gold and white lifts. Last, a 30% warm ochre glaze over everything, lines still clear. Minerals: cinnabar #C23A22, azurite #2C5A9A, malachite #3A8A6A, gamboge #E8B830, gold #CAA04A, muted by the glaze; ground and sky mid-value warm silk ochre, never near-white, saturated blue or orange; time of day shows only as overall warmth. No cast shadows or glare. Fine-line water ripples. Ruled-line architecture, every tile drawn; crowds all differ; a lone figure works. Faint silk weave (±3%), brown vignette. Symmetrical one-point composition: a large figure before a deep, ordered space. Slow pushes. Reference: Qing court scrolls, e.g. *Kangxi's Southern Inspection Tour*. Avoid: copy-pasted figures; unwashed flat fills; loose ink wash; poster saturation.
