English:

Real-time 3D, one unbroken handheld long take, after cinematographer Emmanuel Lubezki (*Birdman*, 2014). A narrow, lived-in passage (default a backstage corridor; an outdoor subject gets its nearest interior, e.g. a stairwell): chipped blood-red enamel dado #6e2a22 on the lower third, stained nicotine plaster #cdb98e above, pipes, cable trays, doors, pinned notices. Light only from practicals every 2–3 m: warm caged tungsten bulbs, one cold flickering fluorescent tube, a red exit sign #ff2a1a; the far door glows. Mid-key: plaster light near lamps, never below mid-grey between them; floor visible. Wide lens (18–24 mm), slow sway (±1°), soft ambient occlusion, light bloom, fine grain (±3%). Over 20–40 s, in any order: lead the subject walking backwards, whip aside to a doorway, let it brush past the lens, turn and follow. Avoid: a locked-off camera, blank walls, repeated textures, a crushed dark frame.

### Director’s Note

Clawd plays Reagan, wearing a slate blue Birdman helmet with a golden beak and wings on both sides. The corridor is a very fast handheld camera, and the drummer's swinging beats are in the frame; the moment when the back door is pushed open, it is stuck on the shooting point, and it cuts to Times Square, and Clawd stops and looks up.

### Storyboards in the finished film

1. Handheld medium shot, the camera walks backwards in front of Clawd wearing the Birdman helmet, pans left to the drummer playing jazz drums in the small room, and then pans back to Clawd walking past the drum set and wiping the camera. (1.3s)
2. Cut to the back door, shot from behind Clawd, with a red EXIT light above his head; the camera moves slightly sideways, Clawd pushes open the door, and there is a white light outside. (0.7s)
3. Cut to Times Square at night: panoramic view, Clawd is standing on the sidewalk, behind him are LED screens; he takes a step, looks up at the screen, and the hand-held lens drifts gently. (0.5s)
